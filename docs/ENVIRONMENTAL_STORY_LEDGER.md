# ENVIRONMENTAL STORY LEDGER — BAY 13: THE SCRAPYARD

**Authority:** Companion to `docs/ARENA_STORY_BIBLE.md`  
**Venue:** **BAY 13: THE SCRAPYARD** (scrap = fight / brawl — not junkyard trash)  
**Rule:** Every major arena element records shipyard past, arena present, dual-purpose visuals, gameplay affect, and activation tag.  
**Activation law:** None of the `interactive later` or inactive foundations become match mechanics without a separate fairness decision.

### Tag definitions

| Tag | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `visual only` | Story / atmosphere; no special match mechanic |
| `interactive later` | Foundation present or planned; gameplay held for fairness |
| `active now` | Currently affects competitive play (usually static collision / floor truth) |

**Gameplay affect:** `Y` = changes match physics, blocking, spawn legality, or (future) hazard state. `N` = presentation / story / atmosphere only.

## H. Fairness & placement law

1. Competitive floor stays clear of trash piles, random debris, and junkyard dump aesthetics.  
2. Story objects live **around, above, or beyond** the fair scrap volume.  
3. `interactive later` and inactive hazard bays stay inactive until a fairness decision covers server state, timing, collision, damage, logging, and replay.  
4. **Scrapyard** naming and **scrap = fight** language apply in all labels, UI copy, and evidence captions.  
5. Free-side title: no Monetaire / Legal Play / cash changes in this ledger’s scope.  
6. No BattleBots or proprietary arena copying.
