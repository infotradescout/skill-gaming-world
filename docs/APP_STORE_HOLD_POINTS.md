# App-store hold points

## Status

No Apple App Store or Google Play submission, approval, rating, billing
approval, or distribution acceptance is established by this repository.
Current policy must be rechecked immediately before every submission.

## Monetaire Play review packet

Before native distribution, document:

- exact product/mode behavior and inaccessible restricted routes;
- Play Coin package mechanics, nonredemption terms, prices, refund/support, and
  restoration behavior where required;
- age rating and minor/privacy treatment;
- account creation, deletion, privacy notice, permissions, and data disclosures;
- public fairness language and absence of fake activity;
- contest/noncash ranking rules;
- reviewer account/environment and truthful review notes;
- storefront/jurisdiction availability and server controls;
- trademark/content licenses and `Monetaire` name clearance.

## Apple-specific questions

Apple's current App Review Guidelines section 5.3 says contests/sweepstakes
require official rules and developer sponsorship, bars in-app purchase currency
used with real-money gaming, and requires licensing and geo-restriction for
real-money gaming. Section 5.1 also addresses privacy and in-app account
deletion. Verify the live policy and obtain counsel/platform confirmation for
the exact product:
[Apple App Review Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/).

Hold points:

- Is the proposed noncash ranking a “contest” for guideline purposes?
- Which official rules and Apple non-sponsorship statement are required?
- Which Play Coin packages must use Apple in-app purchase and how are
  restoration/refund rules met?
- Must Prize and Casino functionality be absent from the binary, remotely
  disabled, or separately distributed?
- What licensing/geolocation evidence would a future mode require?

## Google Play-specific questions

Google Play's gambling/contests policy applies separate requirements to
licensed gambling apps and other real-money games/contests, including
application approval, geography, age, billing, and responsible-gambling
constraints. Verify the current policy for the exact category before submission:
[Google Play real-money gambling, games and contests policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9877032?hl=en).

Hold points:

- Does the exact Play/competition/package design fit an allowed category?
- Is Google Play billing required or prohibited for the proposed transaction?
- What application, license, country, age, geo, rating, and disclosure evidence
  is required?
- Can one binary contain disabled future modes without being treated as a
  gambling product?

## Release rule

Storefront selection and UI concealment cannot replace server denial. Platform
approval does not establish legality, and counsel approval does not establish
platform acceptance. Both are independently required.
