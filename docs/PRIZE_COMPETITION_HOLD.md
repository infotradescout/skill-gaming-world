# Prize competition hold

## Status

`MONETAIRE_PRIZE` is disabled everywhere. No paid entry, valuable prize,
`SKILL_PRIZE_USD` account, payout, redemption, production payment, or prize
promotion is authorized by this repository.

Monetaire Play noncash rankings are not a promise that a future prize mode will
be offered.

## Prohibited initial paths

- Play Coin conversion or spending for entry;
- purchase-linked entries, better odds, bonus prize access, or gameplay
  advantage;
- paid entry of any kind;
- cash, gift card, merchandise, crypto, transferable item, or valuable prize;
- payout/withdrawal endpoints or provider adapters;
- prize balance displayed as coins;
- account or virtual-item resale;
- an alternate method of entry used as a superficial workaround;
- a nationwide “skill gaming is legal” assumption.

## Evidence required to consider release

1. Written feature-by-feature jurisdiction matrix from qualified counsel,
   including consideration, skill/chance treatment, age, exclusions,
   registrations/bonding, rules, tax, advertising, and payment requirements.
2. Approved corporate/operator responsibility and official rules.
3. Separate Skill Prize verification, fresh trusted geolocation, duplicate
   account/fraud screening, sanctions/tax status, and terms acceptance.
4. Prefunded, fixed prize funding and segregated `SKILL_PRIZE_USD` operations
   that cannot touch Play Coins or casino cash.
5. Payment and payout provider approval for exact activities and jurisdictions.
6. Fairness validation, immutable deals/rules, score audit, appeals, support,
   incident, and correction procedures.
7. Privacy, security, tax, responsible-play, app-store/web distribution, and
   marketing approval.
8. Server allowlist, emergency deny, staged rollout, and independent evidence
   review.

The preferred first legal review candidate is sponsor-funded, free-entry,
fixed-prize competition in a deliberately narrow approved jurisdiction set.
That is a counsel question, not an authorization.

## Hold release

Release requires a dated approval packet tied to exact gate keys, code version,
rules version, provider configuration, jurisdictions, and effective dates.
`prize.entry.paid` and `prize.payout` require their own approvals; enabling
`mode.monetaire_prize` does not cascade.

If any evidence expires or conflicts, the server denies new entries. Existing
obligations follow counsel-approved cancellation/refund/payout procedures that
must be documented before entry opens.
