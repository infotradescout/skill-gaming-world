# Brand boundaries

## Names

- Parent/public platform: **Skill Gaming World**
- First game: **Monetaire**
- Recommended title: **Monetaire — Competitive Solitaire**
- Casino: internal placeholder **`CASINO_WORKING_TITLE`**

Do not publish a casino brand name until brand, domain, trademark, social-handle,
and app-store clearance is complete. Monetaire is also a working name until
formal clearance; a web search is not trademark clearance.

## Required public language

Use the complete meaning, prominently and before a Play Coin package action:

> Play Coins have no cash value. Play Coins cannot be withdrawn, transferred,
> sold, or redeemed. Monetaire Play does not award cash or valuable prizes.
> Prize competitions are unavailable unless separately enabled for an eligible
> player and jurisdiction. Casino cash wagering is not currently available.

## Vocabulary

| Use in Play mode | Do not use in Play mode |
| --- | --- |
| Play Coin balance | cash balance, bankroll, funds |
| earn Play Coins | win money, cash winnings |
| package simulation | deposit |
| account history | withdrawal history |
| noncash competition | cash tournament, jackpot |
| ranking/achievement | payout, prize value |

Never use cash, banknote, withdrawal, jackpot, or crypto imagery to imply that
Play Coins hold value. Do not imply that success in simulated play predicts
success in a future real-money product.

## Trust restrictions

- No fake players, winners, live activity, jackpots, prize pools, scarcity,
  testimonials, counters, or “near win” messages.
- No bot may be represented as a human.
- No purchase countdown, false inventory limit, preselected package, or
  intentionally obscured total.
- No claim of fairness certification, independent audit, solvability,
  legal approval, licensing, app-store approval, or payment approval without
  current evidence supporting that exact statement.
- No “MVP” framing in product presentation.

The Casino shell may explain that the product is unavailable. It must not show
playable games, odds, deposits, wallet controls, promotional bonuses, or a
release date not actually authorized.
