# Real-money casino hold

## Status

`REAL_MONEY_CASINO` is a separately governed future operating environment and
is disabled everywhere. The public casino name is not selected; preserve the
internal placeholder `CASINO_WORKING_TITLE`.

There are no authorized:

- casino games or game-provider execution;
- `CASINO_CASH_USD` accounts;
- deposits, wagers, bonuses, withdrawals, or cashouts;
- payment/payout/settlement adapters;
- AML/geolocation/KYC production integrations;
- casino promotions or loyalty value.

Do not scaffold callable placeholder endpoints for these operations.

## Non-negotiable future boundaries

- Licensed operator or approved market-access authority for every location.
- Separate Casino verification with 21+, precise geolocation, identity,
  sanctions/AML, self-exclusion, limits, source-of-funds triggers, and
  casino-specific terms.
- Certified game providers and RNG/outcome controls.
- Segregated player-fund accounting, banking, reconciliation, and regulatory
  access appropriate to the approved model.
- `CASINO_CASH_USD` never receives Play Coins or Skill Prize funds.
- Casino records, terms, accounting, compliance access, and revenue reporting
  are separable from Skill Gaming World operations.
- Passing Skill Prize verification never grants casino access.

## Hold release packet

Counsel and accountable operators must document:

1. entity/operator and each license or market-access partnership;
2. game/provider approvals and allowed catalog;
3. precise jurisdiction/geolocation controls and independent testing;
4. KYC, AML, sanctions, fraud, source-of-funds, and reporting program;
5. self-exclusion, limits, responsible-gaming interventions, complaints, and
   regulatory reporting;
6. banking, restricted-transaction controls, player-fund protection, payment
   approval, reconciliation, and insolvency treatment;
7. tax and record-retention obligations;
8. privacy/security reviews for identity, location, and financial data;
9. official terms, disclosures, marketing, affiliate, and bonus controls;
10. app-store/web distribution approval and incident/kill-switch exercises.

Each released operation uses an exact allowlist and fresh server decision.
Licensure in one place does not imply availability elsewhere.

## Corporate hold

A provisional separation model may place the casino in a separate operating
company or licensed joint venture under a parent intellectual-property owner.
Counsel and accounting professionals must approve entity, service, licensing,
banking, player-fund, tax, and liability arrangements. This document does not
create or recommend a final legal entity structure.
