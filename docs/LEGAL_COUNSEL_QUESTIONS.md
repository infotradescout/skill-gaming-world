# Legal counsel questions

## No legality conclusion

**This repository does not establish that Skill Gaming World, Monetaire, Play
Coin sales, skill competitions, social casino, or real-money casino operations
are legal in any jurisdiction.** It is not legal advice, a license, a
registration, payment approval, or regulatory/app-store acceptance.

Qualified gaming/promotions, privacy, payments, tax, advertising, corporate,
and regulatory counsel must produce a written feature-by-feature matrix. A
general “skill games are legal” answer is insufficient.

## Required matrix format

For every country/state/territory and operation, record:

- `ALLOW`, `DENY`, or `UNRESOLVED`;
- legal authority and effective/review dates;
- operator/entity and license/registration/bond;
- age and physical-location requirements;
- identity, residence, sanctions, tax, and payment requirements;
- official rules/disclosures/marketing limits;
- app-store/web distribution constraints;
- required technical controls and evidence;
- reviewer/approver and next review date.

Unknown, stale, or conflicting answers map to server `DENY`.

## Monetaire Play and Play Coins

1. Which jurisdictions permit the proposed nonredeemable entertainment model
   and package sales?
2. Could rankings, promotions, sponsor events, cosmetics, discounts, account
   resale, virtual inventory, or any fulfillment make Play Coins or outcomes a
   “thing of value”?
3. What age floor and minor/parental-consent rules apply?
4. What consumer disclosures, refund rights, package limits, sales tax, unclaimed
   property, stored-value, auto-renewal, or virtual-currency rules apply?
5. How must noncash competitions and public leaderboards be described?
6. Does the product need simulated-gambling labels, age ratings, or geographic
   exclusions even without redemption?

## Skill competitions and promotions

1. For each state, how is Klondike solitaire classified under predominant
   factor, material element, any chance, or other skill/chance tests?
2. What creates consideration: entry fee, Play Coin purchase/spend, subscription,
   ad view, data collection, task completion, sponsor relationship, or indirect
   purchase benefit?
3. Which free-entry sponsor-funded and paid-entry structures are permitted?
4. Which prize types/amounts, fixed funding, disclosures, escrow/prefunding,
   cancellation, tie, and payout rules apply?
5. Which states/people must be excluded?
6. Are registration, bonding, filings, official-rules deposits, winner lists,
   audit, or regulator notices required?
7. When would a contest be treated as sweepstakes, gambling, or another
   regulated promotion?
8. Would an alternate method of entry change the analysis, and what requirements
   apply? Do not assume it is a solution.
9. What geolocation, identity, duplicate-account, sanctions, payment ownership,
   tax information, and record evidence is required?
10. What age applies? The provisional operational preference is 21+ for
    money-related modes, but counsel must decide.
11. What federal and state tax reporting/withholding, forms, valuation, and
    record retention apply?
12. What official rules, appeal, fraud, correction, and advertising language
    are required?

## Social casino

1. How is each proposed simulated casino game treated by jurisdiction?
2. Do nonredeemability and no prizes resolve the issue, or do consumer
   protection, simulated gambling, age, loss, virtual-currency, or addiction
   rules still apply?
3. Which package, bonus, loyalty, account/inventory, ad, influencer, and
   cross-promotion designs create value or regulatory risk?
4. Which app-store, payment-provider, rating, disclosure, responsible-play, and
   geographic restrictions apply?
5. Can the same Play Coin be used in Monetaire and Social Casino without
   changing its legal/payment treatment?
6. Must social casino be a separate entity, site, binary, terms set, or account
   partition?

## Real-money casino

1. Which licenses or licensed market-access partnerships are required for each
   market and channel?
2. What entity, ownership, suitability, capitalization, banking, player-fund,
   regulatory-access, and reporting structure is required?
3. Which games/providers/RNGs require certification or approval?
4. What KYC, 21+, precise geolocation, AML, sanctions, source-of-funds,
   suspicious-activity, and record requirements apply?
5. Which self-exclusion registries, limits, interventions, disclosures,
   complaint processes, and responsible-gaming reporting apply?
6. What deposit, withdrawal, payment-method, chargeback, bonus, loyalty,
   affiliate, advertising, and tax rules apply?
7. What independent tests and regulator/app-store approvals are required before
   release?

## Privacy, security, and data

1. Which laws apply by residence, physical location, age, and data type?
2. What lawful bases/consents/notices are required for account, device, fraud,
   identity, precise location, sanctions, payment, tax, responsible-play,
   marketing, profiling, and automated decisions?
3. What can be shared between Skill Prize and Casino verification, and what must
   remain separate?
4. What retention/deletion/legal-hold periods apply to identity, location,
   gameplay, ledger, disputes, taxes, exclusions, and audit?
5. What access/correction/export/deletion/appeal rights and response processes
   apply?
6. What vendor, cross-border, security, breach-notification, biometric, and
   minor-data constraints apply?

## Payments, tax, advertising, and distribution

1. Which merchant categories, underwriting disclosures, restricted-transaction
   controls, reserves, chargeback/refund processes, and provider approvals are
   required for each mode?
2. Can app-store billing be used for Play Coin packages? What is prohibited for
   prize/casino operations?
3. What federal/state/local sales, gaming, excise, income, withholding,
   information-reporting, and unclaimed-property treatment applies?
4. Which claims may be made about skill, fairness, solvability, odds, prizes,
   winnings, safety, responsible play, and legal availability?
5. What official rules and platform non-sponsorship statements must appear?
6. Can one app/site contain the four modes, even when server-disabled, or is
   separate distribution required?
